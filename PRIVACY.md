# Privacy notice

GroundsOps AI stores account information, customer and property records, uploaded site photographs, design outputs, quotes, work orders, invoices and operational logs to provide the service. Tenant data is access-controlled and private objects are delivered using short-lived signed URLs.

Production operators must document their legal entity, contact address, hosting region, subprocessors, retention periods and lawful basis before accepting customer data. Customers may request access, correction, export or deletion through the support contact configured by the operator.

Site photographs may contain personal information. Do not upload photographs without authority. Production deployments must not use customer images for model training unless the customer has given separate, explicit consent.

This repository contains a software template and does not itself operate a public service. Replace this notice with legal counsel-reviewed terms before commercial launch.
