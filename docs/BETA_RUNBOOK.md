# Controlled beta runbook

Start with one to three landscaping companies and isolated tenant accounts. Use written consent, non-production test photos where possible and conservative AI budgets. Review every concept, measurement, estimate, quote and invoice with the participant.

Track onboarding completion, generation success rate, protected-area changes, estimate corrections, quote conversion, support incidents and time saved. Hold a weekly review. Stop the beta for tenant-isolation failure, credential exposure, destructive data loss, repeated incorrect billing or uncontrolled AI spend.

Do not describe camera measurements as survey-grade. Record participant feedback and obtain explicit approval before using screenshots or testimonials publicly.
